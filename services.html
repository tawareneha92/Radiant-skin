COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

How many sessions will I need?

Most treatment plans run 3–6 sessions, spaced two to four weeks apart. Your specialist confirms an exact number after your first consultation.

Is there any downtime after treatment?

Most treatments have little to no downtime. Laser and filler procedures may cause mild redness for a few hours, which your specialist will walk you through beforehand.

Do you use organic or clinically tested products only?

Both — organic ingredients are used where they perform best, and clinically tested technology is used for procedures like laser reduction and fillers.

Can I switch membership tiers later?

Yes. You can upgrade or downgrade your membership at your next renewal date with no penalty.

Do you offer treatments for men?

Yes, all treatments are available to every client — several of our hair restoration and skin plans are tailored specifically for men.

Not sure which treatment is right?

A short consultation is all it takes to find out.
