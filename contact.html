Before you book

Do you accept walk-ins?

We recommend booking ahead so your specialist has time reserved, but walk-ins are welcome subject to availability.

What is your cancellation policy?

Please give at least 24 hours' notice to reschedule or cancel a consultation at no charge.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash, all major cards, and UPI. Membership plans can be paid in full or in quarterly installments.

How soon can I get an appointment?

Most consultations are available within 3–5 days. Elite members receive priority booking within 48 hours.
